TimeWinder

A rune that gives the user the ability to control time


Research

Runes are enhancements that add new abilities or buffs to the champions in the League of Legends.

The ability to control time is considered to increase the winning chances of the user.


Inspiration

Ecko uses a combination of Z-Drive and TimeWinder because every second matters when you are in the middle of a fight for survival.

Functionality

Wind it based on the amount of time you need for a task and the rune will let you know when the time is up.


Bill of Materials

Microcontroller

Component Purpose
ESP32-C6Main microcontroller — runs firmware, controls all peripherals

Input

Component Purpose
EC11 Rotary EncoderSets the timer duration by counting CW turns
Cherry MX (MX1A-L1NN)Power on/off button
1P3T Rotary SwitchMode selection — hours / minutes / seconds

Output

Component Purpose
WS2812B 12-LED NeoPixel RingVisualises time remaining — dims one LED per interval
SG90 Micro ServoSweeps needle 0°–180° to show time remaining
CL8530A Passive BuzzerAlarm sound when timer reaches zero

Power

Component Purpose
603450 LiPo 1000mAhMain battery

Passives

Component Purpose
100nF cap × 3Debounce — EC11 CLK, DT, SW lines
100nF cap × 1Debounce — Cherry MX power button
100µF cap × 1Decoupling — NeoPixel VCC rail
100µF cap × 1Decoupling — SG90 servo 5V rail
330Ω resistor × 1Signal protection — NeoPixel DIN line
BC547 / 2N2222 transistor × 1Buzzer driver — GPIO can't source enough current for 16Ω buzzer
1kΩ resistor × 1Base resistor for buzzer transistor
